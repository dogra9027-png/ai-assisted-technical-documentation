AI-Assisted Documentation Review Checklist

Project: Nexus IT Operations Platform
Document Type: Quality Assurance Checklist
Version: 1.0
Status: Portfolio Demonstration

1. Purpose

This checklist provides a structured method for reviewing AI-assisted technical documentation before approval or publication.

It focuses on source accuracy, technical correctness, usability, consistency, and responsible AI use.

2. Source and Requirements Review

  • [ ] Confirm that the correct source documents were used.
  • [ ] Identify the applicable functional requirements.
  • [ ] Check that documented features are supported by approved sources.
  • [ ] Identify conflicting or incomplete requirements.
  • [ ] Record assumptions and unresolved questions.
  • [ ] Confirm that unsupported behaviour has not been presented as fact.

3. Technical Accuracy

  • [ ] Verify technical terminology against approved references.
  • [ ] Check product names and feature descriptions.
  • [ ] Confirm role permissions and access restrictions.
  • [ ] Validate commands, configuration examples, and procedures where applicable.
  • [ ] Remove invented interface labels or behaviours.
  • [ ] Clearly identify conceptual examples that have not been tested.

4. Content Quality

  • [ ] Confirm that the document addresses its intended audience.
  • [ ] Use clear and direct language.
  • [ ] Prefer active voice.
  • [ ] Organize content with logical headings.
  • [ ] Use tables and lists where they improve readability.
  • [ ] Explain technical terms when necessary.
  • [ ] Remove unnecessary repetition.

5. Consistency Review

  • [ ] Follow the documentation style guide.
  • [ ] Use approved terminology consistently.
  • [ ] Maintain consistent capitalization.
  • [ ] Check heading hierarchy.
  • [ ] Confirm that related documents use consistent product terminology.
  • [ ] Check internal links.

6. AI-Specific Review

  • [ ] Identify content that requires human verification.
  • [ ] Check for unsupported AI-generated claims.
  • [ ] Review generated examples for accuracy.
  • [ ] Remove misleading or fabricated details.
  • [ ] Confirm that confidential or unauthorized information was not entered into AI tools.
  • [ ] Retain a record of material human edits where appropriate.

7. Accessibility and Usability

  • [ ] Use meaningful link text.
  • [ ] Provide descriptive headings.
  • [ ] Avoid relying on colour alone to communicate meaning.
  • [ ] Use clear table headers.
  • [ ] Write instructions in a logical sequence.
  • [ ] Ensure that warnings and important notes are easy to identify.

8. Final Review and Approval

  • [ ] Complete editorial review.
  • [ ] Complete technical review.
  • [ ] Resolve or document outstanding questions.
  • [ ] Confirm document status and version.
  • [ ] Obtain required stakeholder approval.
  • [ ] Confirm publication readiness.

9. Review Outcome

Record one of the following outcomes:

Outcome Meaning
Approved Reviewed and approved for publication.
Approved with conditions May proceed subject to documented conditions.
Revision required Changes are required before approval.
Pending validation Additional technical or stakeholder confirmation is needed.

Portfolio note: This checklist demonstrates a proposed quality-assurance process. Its use does not imply that fictional Nexus documentation has undergone real product testing or stakeholder approval.
